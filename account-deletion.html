Request Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play requirements and privacy standards, users who have created an Orbito cloud account can request deletion of their account and associated data directly through this page or inside the mobile app.

01

Delete Directly Inside the App

If you currently have the Orbito app installed on your device, the fastest way to delete your account is inside the app:

  1. Open Orbito on your mobile device.
  2. Tap on Settings (gear icon).
  3. Select Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm your request.

This immediately terminates your account and initiates the removal of your cloud sync records from our Supabase database.
