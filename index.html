Information We Collect

Orbito may process different types of information depending on the features you choose to use. You can use the core functionality of Orbito without creating an account or enabling Cloud Sync.

When you use Orbito without Cloud Sync, information created within the app is stored locally on your device. This may include:

Habit names and descriptions Habit schedules & frequencies Habit completion history Streak records Tasks and to-do items Project information Task due dates & reminder times Focus session durations Focus session history & tags Notes and journal entries Goals & progress information Theme & UI preferences Sound & haptic preferences Local notification schedules

03

Optional Account and Cloud Sync

Orbito may allow you to create an account and enable Cloud Sync. Cloud Sync is completely optional.

If you enable Cloud Sync, information associated with your Orbito account may be transmitted from your device to our backend infrastructure and stored so that your information can be synchronized seamlessly between your authorized devices.

Depending on the features you use, synchronized information may include:

  • Account Credentials: Account email address, user account identifier, authentication metadata.
  • Productivity Data: Habits, tasks, projects, focus sessions, notes, streaks, and completion history.
  • Preferences: Customized settings, notification preferences, and related synchronization tokens.

Cloud synchronization allows you to access your synchronized productivity data whenever you sign in to Orbito on another supported smartphone or tablet.

04

Authentication

Orbito may provide multiple methods of authentication, including third-party providers such as Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In.

When you authenticate using these providers, Orbito receives only the necessary information to authenticate your account:

  • Email address
  • Provider account identifier (UID)
  • Name or basic profile information where provided by the provider

We use authentication information exclusively to:

  • Create or access your Orbito account
  • Verify your identity and maintain your account session
  • Secure access to your synchronized data

We do not use your Google or Apple credentials to access any unrelated services, files, or contacts.

05

Supabase Cloud Infrastructure

Orbito uses Supabase for cloud backend infrastructure, including database hosting and authentication-related functionality where applicable.

When Cloud Sync is enabled, information that you choose to synchronize is processed and securely stored using Supabase infrastructure. Supabase processes this data strictly on our behalf under applicable data processing agreements.

Row Level Security (RLS) Orbito implements database-level Row Level Security (RLS) policies. This ensures that your cloud-synchronized data can only ever be queried or written by your authenticated user session.

For more information about Supabase's privacy and security standards, visit the Supabase Privacy Policy.

06

Biometric Authentication

Orbito may provide an optional App Lock feature using your device's built-in biometric hardware, such as fingerprint or face recognition.

When you use biometric authentication:

  • Authentication is performed directly by the operating system and device security hardware (Secure Enclave / Android Keystore).
  • Orbito does not receive your fingerprint, facial recognition scans, or biometric data.
  • Orbito does not store any biometric templates.
  • Orbito does not transmit biometric information to any server.
  • Orbito only receives a cryptographic boolean result (success or failure) provided by the operating system API.

13

Data Security

We implement comprehensive industry-standard technical and organizational measures to safeguard user data:

  • Transport Layer Encryption: All communications between the mobile application and backend servers use HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Database tables use strict granular access policies ensuring users can only read and mutate their own verified records.
  • Local OS Sandboxing: Local database records reside in your device's protected internal app storage sandbox.
  • Authentication Tokens: Tokens are securely stored using device keystore mechanisms.

While we implement rigorous safeguards, no electronic transmission over the internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% immune from unauthorized breach.

14

Data Retention

Local Data: Stored on your device until you manually delete individual items, clear application data via device settings, or uninstall Orbito.

Cloud Data: Retained for the active duration of your account to provide multi-device sync. Upon account deletion request, your account record and associated cloud entries are permanently deleted from active databases.

Residual copies in automated disaster-recovery backup snapshots are purged in accordance with standard backup cycle expiration (typically within 30 days).

Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, Orbito provides multiple simple ways to request complete deletion of your account and cloud-stored data:

Permanent Action Deleting your account permanently erases your habits, task histories, focus records, and streaks from our synchronization servers. This action cannot be undone.

Method 1: In-App Deletion

Navigate inside the app to: Orbito → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Confirm your decision to immediately purge your account.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form (Google Play Compliant)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, you can submit an account deletion request online without re-installing:

Open Web Account Deletion Page

Method 3: Direct Email Request

Send an email to sumitkumards07@gmail.com (or sumitds700@gmail.com) with the subject line Orbito Account Deletion Request from your registered account email.

16

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When your account deletion is processed, we permanently delete:

  • Your account identity and authenticated user record
  • Your registered email address
  • All synchronized habits, daily check-ins, and completion timestamps
  • All tasks, to-do records, and project lists
  • All focus timer records, sessions, and tags
  • All streak information and notes
  • All cloud synchronization logs

Limited data may remain temporarily in secure backup archives until routinely overwritten (under 30 days). No retained data is used for commercial purposes.

17

Local Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored information anytime without contacting support:

  1. Within Orbito: Clear specific habits, tasks, or use the in-app "Reset Data" option in Settings.
  2. Android System Settings: Open Settings → Apps → Orbito → Storage → Clear Data.
  3. Uninstall: Uninstalling Orbito purges local app storage on modern Android and iOS devices.

Note: Clearing local data does not automatically delete an existing Cloud Sync account. To remove cloud records, use the Account Deletion feature.
