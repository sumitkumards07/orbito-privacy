This Privacy Policy explains how **Orbito** ("Orbito", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, protects, and handles information when you use the Orbito mobile application and related services.

Orbito is a productivity application designed to help you manage habits, tasks, projects, focus sessions, notes, streaks, and personal progress.

Orbito is designed to work **offline-first**. Core features can be used without creating an account or enabling cloud synchronization.

By using Orbito, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect
Orbito may process different types of information depending on the features you choose to use. You can use the core functionality of Orbito without creating an account or enabling Cloud Sync.

### 2.1 Information Stored Locally
When you use Orbito without Cloud Sync, information created within the app is stored locally on your device. This may include:
- Habit names and descriptions
- Habit schedules and frequencies
- Habit completion history
- Streak information
- Tasks and to-do items
- Project information
- Task due dates and reminder times
- Focus session durations and histories
- Tags associated with focus sessions
- Notes and journal entries
- Goals and progress information
- App preferences and theme choices
- Sound and haptic preferences
- Notification schedules
- Other information that you choose to enter into the app

> **Offline Guarantee:** When Cloud Sync is not enabled, this information remains solely on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

---

## 3. Optional Account and Cloud Sync
Orbito may allow you to create an account and enable Cloud Sync. **Cloud Sync is optional.**

If you enable Cloud Sync, information associated with your Orbito account may be transmitted from your device to our backend infrastructure and stored so that your information can be synchronized between your authorized devices:
- Account email address
- User account identifier
- Authentication information
- Habits, tasks, projects, focus sessions, notes
- Streak information and completion history
- Progress information and preferences

---

## 4. Authentication
Orbito may provide multiple methods of authentication (e.g., Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In). Orbito receives only the necessary information to authenticate your account:
- Email address
- Provider account identifier
- Name or basic profile information where provided by the authentication provider

We do not use your Google or Apple account credentials to access unrelated services or files.

---

## 5. Supabase Cloud Infrastructure
Orbito uses **Supabase** for backend infrastructure, including database and authentication-related functionality where applicable.
- Information you choose to synchronize is processed and stored using Supabase infrastructure.
- Orbito uses database access controls, including **Row Level Security (RLS)**, to ensure that users can only access their own synchronized data.
- More info: [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

---

## 6. Biometric Authentication
Orbito may provide an optional **App Lock** feature using your device's biometric authentication (fingerprint or face authentication).
- Authentication is performed directly by the device operating system.
- **Orbito does not receive, store, or transmit your biometric templates.**
- Orbito only receives the verification result (success or failure) provided by the operating system.

---

## 7. Device and Technical Information
Orbito may process limited technical information strictly necessary to operate the application:
- Operating system version and application version
- Device configuration
- Time zone, local date, and time (used for habit schedules, streaks, reminders, and daily resets)
- Network availability status
- Technical crash diagnostics

> **No Location Tracking:** Orbito does not track or store your physical GPS location.

---

## 8. Information We Do Not Collect
Orbito does not require access to or collect:
- Precise GPS location
- Microphone recordings
- Camera images or video
- Contacts or phonebook
- Call history or SMS messages
- Health or medical records
- Biometric templates
- Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking

## 14. Data Retention
- **Local Data:** Retained until you delete entries, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
- **Cloud Data:** Retained while your account is active. Purged upon account deletion request. Disaster backups are overwritten within 30 days.

---

## 15. Account Deletion
You can request deletion of your account and cloud-stored data:
1. **In-App:** `Orbito → Settings → Account → Delete Account`.
2. **Web Page:** [Account Deletion Webpage](https://sumitkumards07.github.io/orbito-privacy/account-deletion.html)
3. **Email:** Contact `sumitkumards07@gmail.com` with subject `Orbito Account Deletion Request`.

---

## 16. What Happens When You Delete Your Account
Upon processing:
- Account profile and email address are purged.
- All synchronized habits, streaks, tasks, notes, and focus logs are permanently erased from active databases.
- Backups are overwritten in routine cycles within 30 days.

---

## 17. Local Data Deletion
- Clear data via device settings: `Settings → Apps → Orbito → Storage → Clear Data`.
- Or uninstall the Orbito app.
